| T-Q6-02 | Tampering | watcher probe + RegisterStatusNotifierItem reply | medium | mitigate | All watcher replies go through godbus typed calls; malformed/unexpected replies surface as errors → the Task 2 one-WARN inert degradation; the daemon never trusts reply content beyond success/failure |
